THE TUG

militia harpoon-tug candidates — spin each model; judged for: workhorse silhouette, harpoon-mount plausibility, lane-fleet kinship · docs/the-hunt.md

The brief: a working boat, not a warship — the hull that drags a surrendered raider to the cold berth. Wants an industrial bow or dorsal spine where a harpoon launcher reads naturally, a strong drive section (it tows for a living), and kinship with the lane fleet's battered-utilitarian look. All candidates downloadable, CC-licensed, sane polycounts.
