# THE FICTION — origin pass 1 (2026-08-14)

Tirtha's ask: our universe is an OFFSHOOT of the Expanse's frame (Earth
→ Mars → the Belt; environment makes peoples), but our story isn't
theirs — we need our own beginning that explains what stands: seven
portfolio systems, the unsurveyed stars, the Drift (and more stations
to come), the Deep ahead, and a visitor in a Rocinante-class. This is
the proposal; his ruling shapes it.

## What the world already says (canon on the ground)

- SURVEY PROBE 7: "DEAD SINCE THE FIRST CHARTS" — a first survey era
  is already canon.
- The inert stars read UNSURVEYED — the charts STOPPED somewhere.
- The Drift is generations-old Belter culture (beratna, beltalowda,
  the militia, the Water Run).
- The billboards are working signage — the colony maintains them.
- BLT-1129 (the player's boat) is a local registry hull; the racing
  club flies the same class.

## THE PROPOSAL — "THE FIRST CHARTS"

Long back, a survey generation came out of the old worlds and CHARTED
THIS POCKET — a handful of stars in a great dark. They strung the
light-boards, sank the docks, named the lanes. The ones who stayed
became the Drift's people: Belters of this pocket, shaped by it.

**The portfolio systems are the ARCHIVE.** The chief surveyor of the
First Charts — the Engineer, T. GHOSH — charted each system and left
its boards carrying one register of his working life: the projects,
the service record, the logs, the letters of the people he worked
beside, what he read, watched, where he walked planetside. The colony
keeps the boards lit not as a museum but as WORKING SIGNAGE — in the
Belt, memory that stops being useful stops being kept. (This is why
the whole neighborhood is, honestly, a portfolio: the world IS his
record, charted in stars.)

**The frontier is where his charts stopped.** KHIONE, SALT, EMBER,
HARROW, VESTIGE — named on approach vectors, never surveyed. The
First Charts ended mid-line. THE DEEP is everything past them — and
the standing invitation of the whole game: the survey is UNFINISHED.
Whoever flies this registry hull (the visitor) is flying his charts —
and one day, past the ash diamonds, continuing them.

**More stations (his new want):** other pockets got other colonies —
sister drifts along other lanes, each with its own docks, boards,
customs, kill-boards, vigils. The Interamnia Drift is simply OURS.
This scales the universe without breaking the frame: every new
station is another community keeping different memory.

## THE JUKEBOX, inside that fiction

Music in the Belt is CARRIED, not composed here. Crews trade tracks
port to port; a song's worth is the distance it traveled. The cantina
box at Amnia Docks is a salvaged sound rig playing tracks off hauler
data cores — every song came in on a ship.

- **NOW PLAYING board**: `"BACKBAY LOUNGE" · OFF THE IMIQ'S CORES` —
  in-fiction attribution to the hauler that carried it, PLUS the real
  CC-BY credit (Kevin MacLeod etc.) beneath. Both lines, always.
- **The switch effect** (Bruno's cassette idea, translated to our
  world): no cassettes in the Belt — a data-core SEEK: a breath of
  radio static, a squelch click, the relay thunk, next track. Same
  comm grammar as the radio chatter; one family of sounds.
- Diegetic point source at the cantina: distance rolloff, ducks under
  the vigil sphere and battle like everything else.
